# A Note on Honesty

Most books about AI are written by people who have never run a business.

This one is written for people who have. The author has spent fifteen years around small companies — service businesses, retailers, agencies, solo operators — and has watched plenty of expensive software promises come and go. AI is different in kind, not just in degree. But it is still a tool, and tools only pay off when they are pointed at real work.

So this book will not tell you the robots are coming. It will not tell you to "embrace the disruption." It will tell you, in plain terms, which parts of your week a machine can do better than you, which parts it absolutely cannot, and how to hand the first kind over without breaking the thing your customers actually pay you for.

Read it with a notebook open. You will find your ten hours.
