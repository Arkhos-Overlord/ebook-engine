## Description (paste into KDP — HTML allowed, this is the formatted version)

<p><b>Stop doing work a machine can do.</b></p>
<p>Most small business owners lose ten hours a week to the same patterned tasks: replying to enquiries, writing quotes, chasing invoices, posting on social media, entering the same data twice. None of it is why you started a business. All of it can be handed to AI - if you know which parts, how, and where to draw the line.</p>
<p>This is not a book about technology. It is a ninety-day operating plan for skeptical, time-poor owners:</p>
<ul>
<li><b>The three-day audit</b> that finds your actual ten hours - and the honest baseline to measure against</li>
<li><b>The prompting skill</b> that turns generic AI output into drafts you would actually send (and the three-question review that catches mistakes before customers do)</li>
<li><b>Ready workflows</b> for enquiry replies, reviews, complaints, social content, quotes, follow-up sequences, invoice chasing and back-office paperwork</li>
<li><b>Your first hands-off automation</b> - built approval-gated, boring, and running by week nine</li>
<li><b>The boundary rules</b> that protect your customers' data and your reputation: what never gets automated, ever</li>
</ul>
<p>No hype, no jargon, no "embrace the disruption." One action per week, sized to fit around a real business. By week twelve you will know exactly what the hours were worth - and what to do with them.</p>
<p><i>If you can send a message and follow instructions, you can run everything in this book.</i></p>
